# Updates

Ultra Front End keeps itself up to date. A new version downloads in the background and installs when you're **not
playing**: when the frontend is closed or left alone for a few minutes. **Settings › About › Update now** installs it
straight away (the frontend closes and opens again on it). Settings › About shows a progress bar while it downloads.

## Channels

Choose in **Settings › About › Updates**:

| Channel | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Main** | Tested releases. The default. |
| **Nightly** | A build of the newest changes, made every night they change, plus the releases. Can have bugs. Choose Main to go back. |

After an update, **News › What's new** shows what changed.

## Emulators

The emulators the frontend installed have their own updates: **Settings › About › Emulator updates**.

## Turning off the usage count

Ultra Front End checks in once a day to show roughly how many people use it. It only counts copies; nothing about you
or your games is sent. Turn off **Settings › About › Count this copy** to opt out.
