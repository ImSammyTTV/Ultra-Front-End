# Add-ons

Small extras you can install from **Settings › Add-ons**. Each one can be removed again, and leaves nothing behind.

| Add-on | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Weather** | The temperature next to the clock, in °C or °F, with the humidity if you want it. |
| **Theme music** | A game's theme plays quietly on its page and fades out when you leave. It goes quiet while a trailer plays or a game is running. Data saver, your own songs, and **Next song** if it picks the wrong one. |
| **Bambu Lab** | How your 3D print is going, in a small card over your game. |

## Writing your own

An add-on is one JavaScript file. In **Settings › Add-ons**, choose **Add-ons folder** and give your add-on a folder of
its own there: it shows up within seconds and reloads every time you save it.

The full guide, with everything an add-on can do, is in [ADDONS.md](../ADDONS.md).
