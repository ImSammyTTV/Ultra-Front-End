# Artwork and game info

## Where it comes from

| What | From |
|---|---|
| Covers | Steam for Steam games; libretro's box art for emulated games; SteamGridDB for the rest, if connected |
| Backgrounds and logos | Steam, Epic, SteamGridDB |
| Description, genre, developer, year | Steam's store page; Wikipedia and Wikidata for games not on Steam |
| Screenshots and trailers | Steam's store page; otherwise an official trailer found on YouTube, with frames from it |
| News | Steam, for Steam games |

Turn it all off with **Settings › General › Find artwork & info online**.

A game with no cover is looked up again every few days, since new games often have no art for their first weeks. If
Wikipedia is busy, the app waits and asks again rather than leaving the description blank.

## Choosing your own (SteamGridDB)

1. **Settings › Accounts › Connect SteamGridDB**, then **Open SteamGridDB**. Sign in (or make a free account) and choose
   **Generate API Key**.
2. Paste the key into the frontend and choose **Connect**.
3. On any game: **Y › Artwork › Change cover** (or logo, or background). Animated covers and backgrounds are marked
   **Animated**.

If the art picker shows the wrong game, choose the right one in the list below the pictures, or search by name. If
SteamGridDB doesn't list a game at all, the picker says so. **Reset cover** (or logo, background) brings back the
automatic one.

Emulated games also get libretro's box art and screenshots to choose from, with no account needed.

## The wrong description?

Use **Y › Rename** and pick the right game from the list. Its details then come from that game's own Wikipedia article.
See [Your games](Your-games.md#renaming-a-game).
