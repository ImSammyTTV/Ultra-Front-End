# Settings

Open **Settings** from the home screen's round buttons.

| Page | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your name, profile icon (or a picture of your own) and time zone. The clock follows the time zone. |
| **Accounts** | Steam, Epic Games, SteamGridDB and RetroAchievements; the store launchers found on this PC. See [Your games](Your-games.md). |
| **Libraries** | Your ROMs folder, and your own folders of PC games, ROMs, single games and folders. See [Your games](Your-games.md). |
| **Systems** | The consoles you play: their emulators and BIOS files. See [Emulators and ROMs](Emulators-and-ROMs.md). |
| **Artwork & info** | Where artwork and game info come from, and SteamGridDB. See [Artwork and game info](Artwork-and-game-info.md). |
| **General** | How the frontend starts and looks (below). |
| **Controllers** | The quick-menu button, the Xbox button, the **Controller tester**, Wii Remote motion from the Steam Controller. |
| **Hidden games** | Games you've hidden: unhide them here. |
| **Add-ons** | Install and set up add-ons. See [Add-ons](Add-ons.md). |
| **About** | Your version, updates and the update channel, emulator updates, the usage count, licences, and **Run the setup guide again**. See [Updates](Updates.md). |

## General

- **Intro** and **Outro:** the opening and closing animations
- **Full-screen game art:** the selected game's background behind the home screen
- **Find artwork & info online**
- **Start with Windows:** open the frontend, start in the background, or not at all
- **Do not disturb while playing:** Windows' notifications stay hidden during a game
- **Keep games on screen:** asks games not to minimise when the in-game menu opens
- **Open with the Xbox button**
- **Use as your wallpaper (Wallpaper Engine)** and **Type on the wallpaper:** see [Wallpaper mode](Wallpaper-mode.md)
- **Installed Steam games** and **Show games that aren't installed**
- **UI scale**, **Platform icons**, **Sounds** and **UI sounds volume**
- **Fullscreen**
- **Rescan everything**
