# Album

Your screenshots and recordings from playing, from the **Album** button on the home screen.

## Taking them (Windows)

In a game started from the frontend:

- **View + RB:** screenshot
- **View + LB:** start or stop recording

The in-game menu has the same.

## A capture's menu (Y)

Press **Y** on a screenshot or video in the Album:

- **Play** / **View**
- **Trim and export** (videos): cut it with the controller, then export the clip
- **Export for sharing:** smaller copies sized for the web, vertical, square and more. **Show in folder** then opens
  where it's saved.
- **Delete:** asks first, then moves it to the Recycle Bin
