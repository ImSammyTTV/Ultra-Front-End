# Ultra Front End wiki

**Ultra Front End** is a fullscreen, controller-first game launcher. Your PC games, emulators and ROMs sit on one
console-style home screen, with a menu you can open over any game.

New here? Start with **[Installing](Installing.md)**, then **[Getting around](Getting-around.md)**.
