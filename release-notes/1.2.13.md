## What's new in 1.2.13

**Privacy & your PC**
- The setup guide has a new Privacy & your PC step: choose whether artwork and info are looked up online, and whether Keep games on screen is on, before either happens
- Keep games on screen changes nothing on your PC until you've been through the setup guide
- Uninstalling also takes away the Keep games on screen setting

**Everything else**
- The app now shows its name and version in its file's Properties › Details
- The project page explains everything the app connects to and changes on your PC, ready for code signing
