## What's new in 1.2.10

**Game info that turns up**
- Rename asks which game it is: pick from Wikipedia's games by that name, and its description, genre, developer, year and trailer come from that article
- Fewer games with no description: a busy moment at Wikipedia no longer leaves them blank for minutes
- A game file named a little differently from its game still finds its description, art and trailer
- A game SteamGridDB had no cover for is looked up again every few days
- The art picker no longer shows another game's art

**Library**
- A progress window while a big batch of games is added
- No more error pop-ups when the internet drops out

**Updates and sound**
- Settings › About shows a progress bar while an update downloads
- Theme music goes quiet while a trailer plays or a game is running

**Emulators and controllers**
- An emulator whose project was renamed installs again
- A cleaner controller picture in the controller tester
